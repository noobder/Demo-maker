How it works

From a folder of code to a finished video in four steps

You stay in control the whole way. Nothing is recorded until you approve the script.

1

Point it at your project

Choose a folder or drop a zip, then describe the video: audience, focus, tone and length.

2

It understands and runs it

The AI reads your files, works out how to start the app, and explores every page like a first-time user.

3

Review the storyboard

Edit every step and every word of narration. Reorder, remove or add steps until it's right.

4

Get your video

A real browser records the app in sync with the voice-over, then adds zoom, captions, music and title cards.

Works with web projects today: static sites, React/Vue/Vite, Flask, FastAPI, Express and more.

Features

Everything a good demo needs

The polish that normally takes a video editor and an afternoon.

Understands your project

Reads your README, config and code to find out what your app is and how to run it, and repairs the plan if it fails.

Real screen recording

Drives your actual app in a browser: clicking, typing sample data and scrolling, so the demo shows what's really there.

Natural voice-over

A script is written and spoken in 17 languages, with free neural voices, OpenAI voices, or fully offline system voices.

Bring your own AI

Run it privately with a local Ollama model, or plug in a Gemini or OpenAI key. Switch any time in Settings.

Professional polish

A visible cursor with click ripples, smooth auto-zoom, burned-in captions, soft background music and title cards.

You stay in control

Every step and line of narration is editable before anything is recorded, and re-rendering is one click.

Private by design

Demo Maker runs on your own computer. Your code and recordings stay here. They only reach a cloud AI if you choose to connect one, and accounts are stored locally, with hashed passwords.
